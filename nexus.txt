Alerts you in chat or with a screen popup if any mods are disabled in DMF. Scans on game load or via /check_disabled_mods.

[b]Options:[/b]

[b]General[/b]
- Enable Notification
- Echo to Chat
- Notification Duration (sec)

[b]Commands:[/b]
- /check_disabled_mods - Scans for what mods are disabled and notifies you.
